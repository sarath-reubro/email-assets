The Solution

One Platform for Your Entire Crane Business

Reubro Cranes is a customised edition of the Reubro Asset Management Solution — purpose-built for crane rental, sales and service companies.

Fleet Management

Every crane in one database — specifications, photos, rental & sale rates, status and full location & movement history.

 

Rental & Sales Management

Clients browse available cranes and request to rent or buy online. Prices, approvals, returns and extensions stay in one flow.

 

Inspection & Calibration

Due dates tracked for every crane. Service orders are auto-generated 14 days before due, with technician assignment and digital certificates.

 

Repair Management

Clients raise repair requests with photos and urgency. Admins assign technicians and clients track every stage.

 

Consolidated Invoicing

Rental, sale, inspection and repair invoices in one list, including pro-rata extension invoices and PDF downloads.

 

Automated Reminders

Automatic email and in-app alerts for inspections, rental return dates and job assignments.
