Apps for Field & Maintenance Teams.

Alongside client work, we build our own apps, so proven solutions are ready to adapt. Two are made for the people who keep equipment running.

 

Reubro Solutions

CMMS

Run maintenance in one app — not paper registers, spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.

A CMMS (Computerised Maintenance Management System) is the one place where every machine, job and spare part is recorded. Instead of fixing equipment only after it breaks, teams plan servicing ahead, cut downtime and always know what was done, by whom and when.

✓  Asset register with QR codes and repair history

✓  Work orders, checklists and requests from any phone

✓  Preventive maintenance scheduling

✓  Spare parts, vendors, POs and live dashboard

Best for

Factories, facilities, hotels, hospitals and schools

Android iOS
 
 

On-Site Productivity

Field Technician App

Everything technicians need on site, in one app for phone or tablet.

Made for technicians who spend the day at customer sites or out on the plant floor, away from a desk. Jobs, equipment details and checklists travel with them, and the office sees progress as it happens — so jobs close faster, return visits drop and every service record is complete. It runs on the phones and tablets your technicians already carry, so there is no extra hardware to buy.

✓  Today’s jobs and on-the-go status updates

✓  Scan barcode or QR for equipment details

✓  Record readings, notes and photos on site

✓  Sync with the office — no duplicate paperwork

Best for

Service, inspection and installation teams
