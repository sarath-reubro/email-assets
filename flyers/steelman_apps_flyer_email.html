Professional‑grade automotive diagnostic tools from STEELMAN — now with dedicated mobile apps for Android & iOS. Detect noise, inspect visually, and test electronics.

From the workshop floor to the assembly line, STEELMAN’s range of diagnostic apps and hardware gives automotive technicians the tools they need to find faults faster, reduce guesswork, and get vehicles back on the road.

SOUND & VIBRATION DETECTION

Locate rattles, squeaks, grinding and vibration in any vehicle, heavy machinery or industrial equipment. Apps in this category connect to STEELMAN’s Wireless ChassisEar hardware via Bluetooth or wired connection and display real‑time audio data in analog or digital formats.

SmartEAR Lite

“Your entry point to professional noise diagnostics.”

Detects sound and vibration in automobiles, heavy machinery & industrial equipment. Displays readings in analog meter, analog waveform, digital numeric or digital bar graph formats. Background sound offsetting, decibel offset and sampling rate are all manually adjustable.

HARDWARE REQUIRED

SmartEAR 1

“A step up with more control and precision.”

All the features of SmartEAR Lite plus a 10‑band frequency equalizer for high‑quality sound detection, selectable audio or vibration input modes, and an on‑board user guide covering sound perception, permissible noise levels, and a sound level comparison reference chart.

HARDWARE REQUIRED

SmartEAR 2

“Multi‑channel diagnostics for complex fault finding.”

Builds on SmartEAR 1 with selectable 6‑channel mode for simultaneous multi‑location diagnostics. Colour‑coded channels with editable position labels make post‑test review straightforward. Record feature captures data during road tests; results can be emailed directly from the app.

HARDWARE REQUIRED

Bluetooth ChassisEAR

“Wireless NVH detection for the modern workshop.”

Steelman Pro Bluetooth ChassisEAR connects wirelessly to the ChassisEar hardware kit via BLE, replacing wired connections entirely. Displays average, peak and real‑time sound level values in analog or digital view. Multi‑band equalizer ensures high‑quality detection even in noisy environments.

HARDWARE REQUIRED

Bluetooth ChassisEAR Lite

“The wireless essentials version — quick & reliable.”

A streamlined BLE version of the ChassisEAR app for technicians who need fast, no‑fuss noise and vibration readings. Supports analog meter, waveform, digital numeric and bar graph views. Includes background sound offset and decibel offset controls for accurate readings in any environment.

HARDWARE REQUIRED

TRAILER & TIRE DIAGNOSTICS

Bluetooth Trailer Tester

“Test your trailer lights solo — from inside the cab.”

Connects via Bluetooth to the SteelMan Pro Trailer Tester hardware. Tests running lights, brake lights, reverse lights, turn signals and the trailer brake link — all without needing a second person at the back of the trailer. Compatible with intelligent trailer modules that monitor output loads. iOS only.

Bluetooth Tire Gauge

“Accurate tire data — all four wheels, one device.”

Scans the vehicle VIN number to automatically determine the correct tyre pressure specification for that vehicle. Measures and reports tyre pressure and tread depth across all four tyres. Results are saved and can be downloaded via email for record‑keeping. iOS only.
